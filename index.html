How it works

    
A social network is a graph: people are dots and friendships are lines between them.

    
Nearest first (BFS)

    
Like ripples in a pond: check all of someone’s friends, then their friends’ friends, and so on. This finds the shortest route between two people.

    
Go deep first (DFS)

    
Like following one lane to its very end, then turning back to try the next. It reaches the same people, in a different order.

    
Friends in common

    
Compare two friend lists and keep the names that appear in both. Friends of friends become suggestions.
